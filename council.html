Candidate statement

GRAY, Jessica Sneha

Certified Clinical Sociologist (AACS) Licensed Investigator Eastern Bay business owner

My principal residence is within the Eastern Bay of Plenty General Constituency area.

Dr Jessica Sneha Gray is a certified clinical sociologist, licensed investigator, and owner of Eastern Bay of Plenty business Mess to Masterpieces Consulting. Her doctorate, research, and professional work focus on how we create strong, sustainable places where people can thrive.

Jessica is not a career politician. She will not be drawn into drama, division, or empty promises. She will listen carefully, speak truth without fear or favour, and work respectfully to achieve the best outcomes for the people she represents.

She may not have a political fan club, but she has the courage, insight, and independence to see what is really happening and act when it matters.

As a multi-ethnic New Zealander with a New Zealand-born husband and children, this is her whenua and her home.

"Strong, sustainable places where people can thrive — that isn't a slogan. It's twenty years of my work."
