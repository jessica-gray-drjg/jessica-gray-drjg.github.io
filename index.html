About Dr Gray

Sharp analysis. Calm, relational presence.

Dr Jessica Sneha Gray is a Certified Clinical Sociologist whose work spans health, finance, academia, media, and complex organisational environments — including Auckland Hospital, ANZ, BNZ, KPMG, and the University of Auckland.

Her doctoral research explored how place shapes personal identity — and how people, in turn, shape place. That lens sits at the heart of every engagement: understanding behaviour in context, without fear, favour, or presupposed judgement.
